About

I'm a student and researcher in Artificial Intelligence based in Ferrara, with a particular interest in symbolic AI, interpretable machine learning and computational logic. I work as a Research Assistant at ACLAI LAB, where I contribute to the development of open-source tools for symbolic learning and rule extraction from complex models, always looking for a balance between formal rigor and practical usefulness.

Outside of academic work I enjoy writing code for lighter, more personal projects: if you'd like a look at that more informal side, you can find it all on my GitHub .
If you need a break ☕, you can also play a round of Circus Charlie.
